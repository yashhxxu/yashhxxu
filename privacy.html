Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information this Discord bot (the "Bot") collects, why, and how it's used. The Bot only operates within Discord servers it has been added to, and only collects information visible to it through Discord's own API.

1. Information We Collect

DataWhy it's collected
Discord user ID, username, avatarIdentifying members for moderation, verification, and leaderboards
Account creation dateUsed during verification to flag very new accounts for staff review (a common anti-raid signal)
Server join dateShown in staff lookup tools
Message countPowers leveling/XP features and activity lookups — message content is not stored long-term, only a running count
Moderation historyWarnings, timeouts, kicks, and bans issued through the Bot are logged so staff can review a member's history

2. What We Don't Do

  • We don't sell, rent, or share your data with third parties.
  • We don't store your message content — only a count of messages sent, for leveling purposes.
  • We don't use your data for advertising.

3. Where Data Is Stored

Data is stored locally on the server operator's own machine, in plain data files used directly by the Bot. It is not uploaded to any third-party analytics or storage service.

4. Automated Message Screening

The Bot checks message text against a standard, open-source list of flagged words to help staff catch messages that may need review. This check happens automatically and briefly; flagged messages are shown to staff for review and are not stored for any other purpose.

5. Data Retention

Data is retained for as long as the Bot remains active in a server, or until a server administrator removes it. If you leave a server, your moderation history and message count remain on file in case you rejoin, so staff have continuity — server staff can request this be cleared.

6. Your Rights

You can ask the staff of any server using this Bot to tell you what data is held about you, or to have it deleted, by contacting server staff directly.

7. Children's Privacy

This Bot operates within Discord and is subject to Discord's own Terms of Service, which require users to meet Discord's minimum age requirement. The Bot does not knowingly collect data from anyone who doesn't meet that requirement.

8. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as the Bot's features change. Continued use of the Bot after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy should be directed to the staff of the server where you encountered the Bot.

This is a template written for a community Discord bot and isn't a substitute for legal advice. If this Bot is used commercially, handles data for EU/UK users, or operates at large scale, consider having this policy reviewed by a lawyer for GDPR/CCPA compliance.
